Welcome to Vrindavan

Where devotion meets divinity – a sacred land of Lord Krishna.

The Ancient Glory

Vrindavan, located in Uttar Pradesh, India, is a city deeply rooted in the stories of Lord Krishna. It is said that every dust particle here holds divine blessings. Thousands of temples, ghats, and sacred ponds narrate the tales of Krishna’s childhood and his eternal love for Radha.

Spiritual Attractions

From the enchanting Banke Bihari Temple to the mesmerizing Prem Mandir, Vrindavan offers an unforgettable spiritual journey. The evenings filled with bhajans, aarti, and chants create an atmosphere of pure devotion and peace.

Culture & Festivals

Vrindavan celebrates festivals like Holi and Janmashtami with unmatched enthusiasm. Streets come alive with music, colors, and devotion. The city’s cuisine, art, and lifestyle reflect simplicity, joy, and spirituality.
